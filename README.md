# Dr. Qing Wang (王卿)

**Honorary Research Associate** *Department of Statistics and Actuarial Science, The University of Hong Kong (HKU)*

---

## 🧬 Professional Profile
Dr. Qing Wang is a researcher specializing at the intersection of **Large-scale Data Mining**, **Causal Inference**, and **AIOps (Artificial Intelligence for IT Operations)**. Her work focuses on building intelligent, self-healing systems and advanced recommendation frameworks. 

Much of her research was pioneered during her tenure with **IBM Research**, where she played a key role in developing the causal learning engines for **Watson AIOps**.

## 🚀 Research Interests

### 1. Causal Discovery & System Reliability
Focuses on identifying "cause-and-effect" relationships in complex cloud architectures.
* **Root Cause Analysis (RCA):** Developing algorithms to localize faults in microservices.
* **Interventional Learning:** Using fault injection to improve the robustness of causal models.
* **Golden Signal Monitoring:** Leveraging latency, traffic, and error metrics for predictive maintenance.

### 2. Multi-armed Bandits & RL
Advancing decision-making under uncertainty for interactive environments.
* **Online Collaborative Filtering:** Modeling dependent arms to improve recommendation accuracy.
* **Exploration-Exploitation:** Balancing user satisfaction with system learning in real-time.

### 3. Scalable Data Mining
* **Unstructured Log Mining:** Transforming raw system logs into actionable insights.
* **Automated Service Management:** Reducing manual intervention in large-scale IT infrastructure.

## 🏆 Key Achievements
* **IBM Research Accomplishment Award (2021):** For contributions to Causal Learning.
* **IBM Outstanding Technical Achievement Award:** For work on Watson AIOps.
* **Best Student Paper Award:** IEEE International Conference on Services Computing (SCC), 2017.
* **Patents:** Holder of multiple US patents in causal inference and automated data mapping.
